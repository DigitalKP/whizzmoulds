Should I choose an aluminium mould or a steel mould?

Aluminium is lighter, transfers heat quickly and is generally the lower-cost option, which suits PU and dip moulding and small to mid batches. Steel is heavier and is usually chosen where higher durability is needed, such as rubber, PVC or very high-volume work. The decision should be made against your actual application.
