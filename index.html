ABOUT WHIZZ MOULDS

KANPUR’S TRUSTED MOULDING TOOLHOUSE SINCE 2015

Whizz Moulds is a leading sole mould manufacturing proprietorship based in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh — the heart of India's leather and footwear industry. Led by Proprietor H. Verma, we specialize in high-precision aluminium, steel, and polymer moulds for footwear factories.

Our workshop combines 3D CAD patterning, CNC engraving, and hands-on finishing to ensure every mould pair delivers clean release, zero flash lines, and long operational lifespan under continuous industrial production.

ENGINEERING FLOW

4-STEP MANUFACTURING PROCESS

From technical blueprint drawing to trial sample pouring, every mould passes strict quality gates.

01

CAD TOOLING DESIGN

Converting client footwear soles, tread patterns, and sizing specifications into 3D CAD toolpath designs.

02

CNC MACHINING

Precision milling on high-grade aluminium blocks or forged steel blocks using multi-axis CNC engravers.

03

SURFACE FINISHING

Hand polishing, sandblasting texture application, and ejector pin fitting to ensure smooth release during pouring.

04

SAMPLE TRIAL

Test pouring and dimensional accuracy verification before final packing and client delivery in Kanpur.
