FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

COMMON QUESTIONS ABOUT SHOE SOLE MOULDS

What is a shoe sole mould used for?

A shoe sole mould is used to form a sole according to a defined product design, size, tread pattern and production requirement. It helps manufacturers produce consistent sole shapes during regular production.

Which is better for shoe soles, aluminium or steel?

The suitable material depends on the product, manufacturing method, production volume and required durability. Aluminium is lighter and transfers heat quickly, while steel is heavier and is generally selected where higher durability is required. The right choice should be discussed against the actual application.

How much does a custom shoe sole mould cost?

There is no single fixed price for every custom shoe sole mould. Cost can vary with design complexity, material, cavity count, size range and order quantity. Share your product details with Whizz Moulds for a project-specific quotation.

Can you make a mould from my sample or 3D file?

Yes. You can share a physical sample, sketch, drawing or 3D/CAD file to discuss the mould requirement. Product dimensions, tread pattern, logo and other design details can also be considered during the mould planning process.

Do you make moulds for PU, PVC, rubber and slipper soles?

Whizz Moulds provides moulding solutions covering PU, PVC and rubber applications, along with footwear and slipper moulding requirements. The appropriate mould configuration depends on the product, material and manufacturing process.
