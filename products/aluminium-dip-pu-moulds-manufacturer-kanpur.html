WHY ALUMINIUM FOR DIP MOULDING?

Aluminium can be a practical choice where light weight, heat transfer and easy handling are important.

01

LIGHTWEIGHT

Aluminium is easier to handle than heavier mould materials in suitable production applications.

02

03

PU MOULDS

PU MOULDS FOR POLYURETHANE PRODUCTION

Custom moulds planned around your polyurethane product, dimensions and production requirement.

PU moulding uses liquid polyurethane that is introduced into a mould and allowed to set into the required shape. A suitable mould helps maintain consistent product dimensions and surface finish from one piece to the next.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

COMMON QUESTIONS ABOUT ALUMINIUM DIP MOULDS AND PU MOULDS

How is an aluminium dip mould made?

It starts with your drawing, sample or CAD file. The design is reviewed, the mould is manufactured to the required dimensions, finished and checked before delivery.

What are aluminium moulds made of?

Aluminium moulds are made from aluminium or suitable aluminium alloys. The appropriate grade depends on the product, process, production quantity and application.

How much does an aluminium dip mould cost?

Cost depends on the design, size, complexity, material and production requirement. Custom moulds are quoted according to the individual project rather than one fixed price.

What is PU moulding?

PU moulding is a process in which liquid polyurethane is introduced into a mould and allowed to set into the required shape. The mould is designed around the product dimensions and finish.

What information do I need for a mould quote?

Share a drawing, CAD file, physical sample, photographs or dimensions, along with material and expected production information where available.
